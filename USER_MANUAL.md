# Analog Media Tracker User Manual

Open [Analog Media Tracker](https://gwinnc.github.io/AnalogMediaTracker/) in a web browser. No account is needed.

## Your collection and your data

Your records are saved in the browser on the device you use. They are not synced between devices or shared with other people. If you clear this browser's site data, you may lose your collection. Export backups regularly and keep them somewhere safe.

## Add or edit a record

1. Open **Add a record**.
2. Enter the artist and album name. Both are required.
3. Optionally add up to four album-art images. The first image is the cover. Choose **Make cover** under another image to change it. Select × to remove an image, or select an image to view it larger.
4. Add track details as needed: side, track number, song title, and issue or scratch notes. Choose **Add Another Track** to add more tracks.
5. Add any other details you want, such as replacement status, vinyl and sleeve condition, genre, storage location, release date, pressing year, price paid, or owner.
6. Choose **Save Record**.

To edit a record, choose **View / Edit** in Table or Gallery view. Make your changes and save them. Choose **Cancel Edit** to leave without saving.

## Browse your collection

- Switch between **Table**, a list of records, and **Gallery**, album cards with cover art.
- Select a cover image to open it larger.
- Use **Sort by**, **Filter by genre**, **Filter by owner**, and **Replacement filter** to sort or narrow the list. Search checks artist, album, genre, owner, storage location, track titles, and issue notes.
- When sorted by **Artist (A–Z)**, use the A–Z buttons to jump to an artist. In mobile Gallery view, the letter menu is beside the cards on the right.
- **Shopping view** is available in Table view. It hides some details while you shop; choose **Show full collection** to return.
- Use the **Dark mode** switch to choose a theme, or choose **Use system** to follow your device setting.

## Delete a record

Choose **Delete** and confirm. You can choose **Undo** within 10 seconds. After that, restore the record from a backup if you have one.

## Back up or move your collection

- Choose **Export Backup** to download a backup of all records.
- To back up only one owner's records, select that owner in **Filter by owner**, then choose **Export Selected Owner**.
- The JSON backup includes records and album-art images. Keep it somewhere safe; it may contain personal information.
- To move records to another device, transfer the backup file there, select it under **Restore from backup file**, review the preview, and choose **Restore backup**.
- Restoring adds records not already present and replaces records with matching IDs. It does not remove records absent from the backup. Records with matching contents are skipped.

## Add the app to your device

Adding an icon creates a shortcut to the website; it does not sync your collection between devices.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open the app in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the upward arrow).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**. You may need to scroll the share options.
4. If offered, turn on **Open as Web App**, then tap **Add**.
5. Open Analog Media Tracker from the new Home Screen icon.

### Android phone or tablet

1. Open the app in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then confirm.
4. Open Media Tracker from the Home Screen.

Menu wording varies by device and browser. If **Install app** is not shown, choose **Add to Home screen**.

### Windows

- In Microsoft Edge, open the app menu and choose **Apps** > **Install this site as an app**.
- In Chrome, look in the browser menu for **Install page as app** or **Create shortcut**.

### Mac

- In Safari on macOS Sonoma or newer, open the app and choose **File** > **Add to Dock**.
- In Chrome, use **Install page as app** in the browser menu if available.

## Updates and offline use

Open the app while online from time to time so it can receive updates. If it looks out of date, close it completely and reopen it while online.

After the first successful online visit, the app may remain available offline. Keep a backup even if you use it offline.
