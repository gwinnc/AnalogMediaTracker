# Analog Media Tracker User Manual

Open the app at [https://gwinnc.github.io/AnalogMediaTracker/](https://gwinnc.github.io/AnalogMediaTracker/).

## What the app stores

The app keeps your collection in the browser on the device you are using. It does not require an account, and the app does not upload your collection to GitHub Pages or sync it with other people. Each person—and each browser or device—has a separate collection.

Because the collection is stored locally, export backups regularly. Clearing the browser's site data or changing devices can make the collection unavailable unless you have a backup.

## Add a record

1. Open **Add a record**.
2. Enter the artist and album name. These are required.
3. Optionally add up to four album-art images. The first image is the cover. Use **Make cover** below another image to change it. Select the × control to remove an image; click an image to view it larger.
4. Add track details as needed: side (A–D), track number, song title, and issue or scratch notes. Use **Add Another Track** for more tracks.
5. Add any other details you want, such as replacement status, vinyl and sleeve condition, genre, storage location, release date, pressing year, price paid, or owner.
6. Select **Save Record**.

To change an existing record, choose **View / Edit** in Gallery or Table view. Make your changes and save. Choose **Cancel Edit** to leave without saving.

## Browse your collection

- **Table** shows the collection as a list. **Gallery** shows album cards and cover art. Choose either view with the toggle above the collection.
- Select a cover image to open the larger image viewer.
- Use **Sort by**, **Filter by genre**, **Filter by owner**, and **Replacement filter** to narrow or reorder the collection. Search checks artist, album, genre, owner, storage location, track titles, and issue notes.
- When sorted by **Artist (A–Z)**, use the A–Z buttons to jump to an artist. In mobile Gallery view, the letter menu is on the right beside the cards.
- **Shopping view** is available in Table view. It hides some extra details to make the list easier to use while shopping; select **Show full collection** to return.
- Use the **Dark mode** switch to choose a theme, or select **Use system** to follow your device setting.

## Delete a record

Choose **Delete** and confirm. An **Undo** option appears for 10 seconds after deletion. If that notice disappears, use a backup to restore the record.

## Back up or move your collection

- Choose **Export Backup** to download a backup of the full collection.
- To export only one owner's records, first select that owner in **Filter by owner**, then choose **Export Selected Owner**.
- Keep the downloaded JSON backup somewhere safe. It contains the records and album-art images; do not share it unless you intend to share that information.
- To load a backup on another device, transfer the backup file to that device, choose it under **Restore from backup file**, review the preview, and select **Restore backup**.
- Restore adds new records and updates records with matching IDs. It does not delete records that are absent from the backup, and it skips records whose contents already match.

## Add the app to a device

Adding the app to a home screen or desktop creates a shortcut to the same website. It does not sync collections between devices.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the upward arrow).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**. You may need to scroll the share options.
4. If offered, turn on **Open as Web App**, then tap **Add**.
5. Open **Analog Media Tracker** from the new Home Screen icon.

### Android phone or tablet

1. Open the app link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then confirm.
4. Open **Media Tracker** from the Home Screen.

The wording can vary by Android version and browser. If **Install app** is not shown, use **Add to Home screen**.

### Windows computer

- In Microsoft Edge, open the app menu and choose **Apps** > **Install this site as an app**.
- In Chrome, open the browser menu and look for **Install page as app** or **Create shortcut**. Menu wording can vary by version.

### Mac

- In Safari on macOS Sonoma or newer, open the app and choose **File** > **Add to Dock**.
- In Chrome, use the browser menu's **Install page as app** option if available.

## Updates and offline use

Open the app while online from time to time so it can receive updates. If it looks out of date after an update, close the app completely and reopen it while online.

After the first successful online visit, the app may remain available offline. Keep a backup even if you use it offline.
