ANALOG MEDIA TRACKER

User Guide

A quick guide to tracking your record collection.

Back to collection

Open the app at gwinnc.github.io/AnalogMediaTracker. Your collection is stored in your browser on this device.

Your collection and privacy

You do not need an account. The app does not upload your collection to GitHub Pages or sync it with other people. Each person—and each browser or device—has a separate collection.

Clearing browser site data or changing devices can make your collection unavailable unless you have a backup. Export backups regularly.

Add and edit records

  1. Open Add a record.
  2. Enter the artist and album name. Both are required.
  3. Optionally add up to four album-art images. The first image is the cover. Choose Make cover under another image to change it. Select × to remove an image, or select an image to view it larger.
  4. Add track details as needed: side (A–D), track number, song title, and issue or scratch notes. Choose Add Another Track to add more.
  5. Add any other details you want: replacement status, vinyl and sleeve condition, genre, storage location, release date, pressing year, price paid, or owner.
  6. Choose Save Record.

To change a record, choose View / Edit in Table or Gallery view, make your changes, and save. Choose Cancel Edit to leave without saving.

Browse your collection

  • Switch between Table, a compact list, and Gallery, album cards with cover art.
  • Select a cover to open the larger image viewer.
  • Sort by artist, album, pressing year, genre, recently added, or recently edited. Filter by genre, owner, or replacement status.
  • Search checks artist, album, genre, owner, storage location, track titles, and issue notes.
  • When sorted by Artist (A–Z), use the letter menu to jump to an artist. In mobile Gallery view, the menu is on the right beside the cards.
  • Shopping view is available in Table view. It hides some extra details; choose Show full collection to return.
  • Use the Dark mode switch to choose a theme, or Use system to follow your device setting.

Delete a record

Choose Delete and confirm. An Undo option appears for 10 seconds. If it disappears, use a backup to restore the record.

Back up or move your collection

  • Choose Export Backup to download all records. To export one owner's records, first select that owner in Filter by owner, then choose Export Selected Owner.
  • Keep the downloaded JSON backup somewhere safe. It contains your records and album-art images; do not share it unless you intend to share that information.
  • To load a backup on another device, transfer the file to that device, choose it under Restore from backup file, review the preview, and choose Restore backup.
  • Restore adds new records and updates records with matching IDs. It does not delete records absent from the backup, and it skips records whose contents already match.

Add the app to a device

Adding the app to a home screen or desktop creates a shortcut to the same website. It does not sync collections between devices.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the app link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the upward arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. If offered, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  5. Open the new icon from your Home Screen.

Android phone or tablet

  1. Open the app link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.
  4. Open the new icon from your Home Screen.

Menu wording varies by browser and device. If Install app is not shown, choose Add to Home screen.

Windows computer

  • In Microsoft Edge, open the app menu and choose Apps > Install this site as an app.
  • In Chrome, look for Install page as app or Create shortcut in the browser menu.

Mac

  • In Safari on macOS Sonoma or newer, open the app and choose File > Add to Dock.
  • In Chrome, use the browser menu's Install page as app option if available.

Updates and offline use

Open the app while online from time to time so it can receive updates. If it looks out of date after an update, close the app completely and reopen it while online.

After the first successful online visit, the app may remain available offline. Keep a backup even if you use it offline.
