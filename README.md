# Analog Media Tracker

Analog Media Tracker is a free, browser-based tool for organizing a vinyl record collection. Open the [app](https://gwinnc.github.io/AnalogMediaTracker/) to start; no account or installation is required.

## What you can do

- Record artist, album, tracks, condition, pressing details, storage location, price, owner, and notes.
- Add album-art images.
- Browse records in a table or gallery, and sort, filter, or search your collection.
- Create JSON backups and restore them on another device.
- Use the app on desktop or mobile, including as a home-screen shortcut.

## Your collection and privacy

Your collection is stored in your browser on your device. It is not synced to other devices or shared with other people. Clearing the browser's site data may remove your records, so export backups regularly and keep them somewhere safe.
